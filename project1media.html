Our Coffee Experience

Coffee is more than a drink. It is an experience made from carefully selected beans and expert brewing.

From Bean to Cup

We select quality coffee beans to prepare every cup. Our beans are roasted in small batches so that each cup has a fresh and balanced flavour.

How We Prepare Coffee

  1. Select fresh coffee beans.
  2. Grind the beans to the correct size.
  3. Choose the appropriate brewing method.
  4. Serve the coffee fresh and hot.

Coffee Facts

Espresso
A small, strong shot made by forcing hot water through fine grounds.
Cappuccino
Equal parts espresso, steamed milk and milk foam.
Cold Brew
Coffee steeped in cold water for 12 hours for a smooth, sweet taste.
Arabica Beans
Sweeter and softer in flavour. Used in most of our drinks.
